ΔVR–RC TR Malignancy Risk Calculator

This tool was developed from a multivariable logistic regression model for malignancy risk prediction in thyroid nodules. After entering ΔVR and RC TR, the calculator automatically generates an individualized linear predictor, the predicted probability of malignancy, and the corresponding dual-threshold risk classification.

2. Model equation

Linear predictorLP = −10.379 − 0.063 × ΔVR + 2.983 × RC TR

Logistic transformationScore = 1 / [1 + exp(−LP)]
LP denotes the individualized linear predictor. The score denotes the predicted probability of malignancy, also referred to as the ΔVR–RC TR Malignancy Score.

5. Scope and limitations

This calculator is intended only to support malignancy risk stratification in thyroid nodules and should not be used as a stand-alone diagnostic tool. Model outputs should be interpreted together with clinical information, conventional ultrasound features, TI-RADS classification, and cytological or histopathological results. Before real-world clinical implementation, external validation is recommended to further assess discrimination, calibration, and clinical net benefit.

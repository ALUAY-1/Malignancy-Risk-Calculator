ΔVR–RC TR Malignancy Score Calculator

This exploratory calculator applies the fixed multivariable logistic regression equation to derive an individualized predicted probability of thyroid nodule malignancy. The two thresholds are displayed as development-derived exploratory thresholds and should not be interpreted as established or validated clinical cutoffs.

2. Model equation

Linear predictorLP = −10.379 − 0.063 × ΔVR + 2.983 × RC TR

Logistic transformationScore = 1 / [1 + exp(−LP)]
LP denotes the individualized linear predictor. The score denotes the predicted probability of malignancy derived from the logistic transformation.

5. Scope and limitations

This calculator is intended for research-use presentation of the fixed ΔVR–RC TR logistic model. The displayed thresholds are development-derived exploratory thresholds, not established clinical decision cutoffs. The model output should not be used as a stand-alone diagnostic result and should be interpreted together with conventional ultrasound findings, TI-RADS category, clinical information, and cytological or histopathological results. Independent validation and clinical impact assessment are required before real-world clinical implementation.
